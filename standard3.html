18. Business and Industry Membership

Document the business and industry members of the advisory committee. More than half of the committee should consist of business and industry representatives.

Required representation

  • At least one local Chamber of Commerce member
  • At least one SHRM member or human resources professional
  • At least one business or industry representative from each pathway offered
  • Committee officers, including the business co-chair and secretary

21. Business and Industry Needs Assessment

Document the local business and industry needs assessment. The survey should be sent to at least 50 business and industry representatives, with at least 25 responses collected.

Survey topics

  • Entry-level positions available in the community
  • Use of employment tests for entry-level positions
  • Types of employment tests required
  • Technical skills needed by entry-level employees
  • Software used by local businesses
  • Soft skills needed for employee success
