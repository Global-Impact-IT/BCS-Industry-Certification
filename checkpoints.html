Global Impact Academy Industry Certification

Business and Computer Science Program

This website contains documentation for the 2026-2027 Business and Computer Science Industry Certification process.

Checkpoint 1: October 1

The sections below identify the documentation required for the first checkpoint. Add the approved evidence files to the matching standard page.

Standard 1: Curriculum and Instruction

  • Course syllabus for every first-semester course
  • Pacing guide for every first-semester course
  • At least one FBLA activity for each course and class period
  • Proposed long-term project for each pathway
View Standard 1 evidence

Standard 2: School, Program, and Teachers

  • Teaching certificate for each teacher
  • Daily schedule, workshop date, experience, and additional responsibilities
  • ACTE, GACTE, and GBEA membership documentation
  • NBEA and SBEA membership documentation
  • Professional development and job-shadow documentation
View Standard 2 evidence

Standard 3: Advisory Committee and Community Involvement

  • Business, industry, and education membership chart
  • Local business and industry needs survey
  • Advisory committee activity log
View Standard 3 evidence

Standard 4: Employability Skills and Career Development

  • At least one employability-skills activity for each course and period
  • At least one career-development activity for each course and period
  • Assignment sheet, graded student sample, and teacher feedback for each activity
View Standard 4 evidence

Standard 5: Program Facilities

  • Annual Business and Computer Science program budget
  • Industry Certification grant budget, if applicable
View Standard 5 evidence

Standard 6: Future Business Leaders of America

  • FBLA Program of Work
  • Chapter meetings and activities completed to date
  • Elected official speaker documentation, when completed
  • Community and school service project documentation
View Standard 6 evidence
