26. Employability-Skills Activities

Document multiple employability-skills activities for every course and class period taught by each teacher. Activities should vary in format and depth based on student grade level.

Possible employability skills

  • Written, verbal, listening, reading, and interpersonal communication
  • Creativity and innovation
  • Critical thinking and problem-solving
  • Email etiquette and professional communication
  • Integrity, honesty, accountability, and punctuality
  • Time management and respect for others
  • Independent work and teamwork
  • Professional appearance, behavior, and language
  • Job applications, resumes, and cover letters

28. Career-Development Activities

Document at least two career-development activities for every course and class period taught by each teacher. Activities should vary based on course content and student grade level.

Possible career-development activities

  • Student job-shadow experiences and reflections
  • Career research projects
  • Career-focused field trips and student reflections
  • Mock interviews with completed rating sheets
  • Job fairs
  • Professional organization involvement
  • Postsecondary education and training research
  • Career pathway and credential research
